## Our Mission

Graduate Texts in Minecraft (GTMC) is an open knowledge base for the
technical side of Minecraft. We publish tutorials, mechanics explanations,
and annotated source-code readings, all written and reviewed by the
community.

We follow the example of an academic monograph series: rigorous, structured
writing that covers the whole stack of technical play, from production farms
and redstone engineering to the tick-by-tick details of the engine.

## Contribute

GTMC grows through community contributions. Draft and submit articles on
the site, or help build the codebase.

- **Write an Article** — Draft, preview, and submit articles through the built-in editor.
- **Contribute Code** — The website is open source. Bug reports, improvements, and new features are welcome.
